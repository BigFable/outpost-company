# Playtest 1 — owner and friends (2026-10-24)

Build: Outpost Company Playtest (v1 and v2 files). Feedback from the owner, relayed for one hardcore and one casual friend.

## Bucking (Plan the cuts)
- Needs clearer logic for relaxed ("vibing") players. The hardcore player figured it out and took advantage; the casual friend needed more help.

## Hauling (Drive a load)
- Weakest mini-game. Needs a better improvement and perk system.
- The track sometimes starts with a stall-out.
- Getting fined for a max load is not cool. A harder, heavier run should be rewarded, not punished.
- Par time should be set from the average achievable speed on the current track, plus a margin.

## Skidding (Skid a turn)
- Wants a legendary perk that turns mud into a boost.
- Wants improvements that let you skid huge trains of logs.

## Felling (Place a fall)
- Amazing; perks feel perfect here.
- Domino fall was a huge upgrade: it pushed one player to an early prestige with over $1M in an hour.

## Progression
- After the first prestige the game should widen: unlock a new skill (camp type), with progressive item drops from the logging camp (e.g., fine logs) used to improve other camps — for example, building a fishing dock or fishing poles.
