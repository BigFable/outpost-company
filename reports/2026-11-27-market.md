# Market watch: 27 November 2026

New since 20 Nov only. Gaps this week: the Click to Medieval Roguelite store page, the mobile idle roundup and the Steamworks events page could not be opened, so first reviews of Click to Medieval and new mobile launches are unchecked.

## Melvor Idle 2, week two: still growing
- Reviews rose to **Overwhelmingly Positive, 96% of 1,544** English reviews (last week: Very Positive, about 91% of 564).
- Players: about **24,000 concurrent** on 27 Nov. Launch day peaked near 19,000; the week-one peak was about 30,000, so it is holding at roughly 80% of peak. Best Top Sellers rank so far is #11.
- Price is back to the full **$14.99** (UK £12.99); the launch discount ended 24–25 Nov.
- First patch, **v0.11.7-alpha (24 Nov)**: quality-of-life fixes (bank tab icons, tooltips, Steam Deck controls). The post says the Early Access roadmap is due "this week". Android and iOS follow in 2027.

## Other competitors
- **Idle Clans:** nothing new this week. Its last big update found (the Invocation skill, a relic-exploration skill feeding combat rituals) dates from March.
- **Incremental releases on Steam:** no new notable incremental launches 15–30 Nov in Incremental Atlas's 2026 list. The newest is **PlinkIdle** (2 Nov, $7.99, ball-drop clicker with prestige). The list shows most 2026 premium incrementals priced **$4–10**, many as short games with an ending (Incremental Orbits $6.99, Rushcremental $5.49). **Rocky Idle** ($9.99, skill-based idle game with 18 activities) is the nearest skilling comparison.

## Implications for Outpost Company
1. **Melvor 2 is stronger than it looked last week.** It now holds 24,000 concurrent players with 96% positive reviews. The skilling-idle audience is bigger and more engaged than "loyal but modest" suggested. Wishlisting from Melvor fans is worth more effort. Keep "RuneScape" and "Melvor" out of our own titles and art.
2. **$7.99 sits in the middle of the premium incremental range ($4–10).** It is well below Melvor's $14.99. No change is proposed; the price remains the owner's call.
3. **Watch Melvor's Early Access roadmap, due this week.** If it includes anything roguelite-like (runs, resets, drafted modifiers), our "every skill is a run" pitch gets less room. Check it in next week's report.
