# Market watch — 30 October 2026

Covers only what is new since the 23 October watch.

## Melvor Idle 2 has a date and a RuneScape name
- Announced 29 Oct: the sequel is now **Melvor Idle 2: A RuneScape Adventure**, made with Jagex. It is not set in RuneScape's world; the subtitle points to its skilling roots.
- **Early Access starts 17 November 2026** on Steam. No price announced yet. The store page says 12–18 months in Early Access and the price will not rise at 1.0.
- Scope: 29 skills (5 new), level cap 120 (DLC raises it), quests, combat, dungeons, "new game modes", mod support, cloud save across PC and mobile (mobile later), no microtransactions.
- Offline progress runs **at the same speed as active play**. Still no run or draft features mentioned.

## New and upcoming releases
- **10 Minutes to Make a Million** (Steam, this week): a roguelike economy game where you draft businesses against a 10-minute timer. It is another run-plus-draft incremental, though not a skilling one. No reception data yet.
- **Snakecremental** is out on Steam: a Snake game with a skill tree. **Idle Eternum** (iOS/Android, early November) uses time travel around a black hole as its hook.
- This week's idle releases on Steam (about 51) are mostly idle RPGs and miners: Mining Guild Idle, Endless Battle: Idle & Build (8 skills), Ember Reaches (gather and craft). Babel Legacy, a city-building idler, is the only one with a solid early score (89% of 238 reviews).
- Nothing new from Idle Clans. The mobile roundup page failed to load again, so mobile coverage this week is thin.

## Trend
Solo developers still dominate the idle scene, mostly with Steam demos, Discord communities and Early Access. One veteran solo idler (Degens Idle) missed its 1.0 target and is only now resuming work. That is a reminder that idle players notice when a schedule slips.

## Steam calendar (no changes; new deadlines)
Auto-Battler RPG Fest 16–23 Nov · Winter Sale 17 Dec – 4 Jan · Desktop Companion Fest 14–18 Jan · Shop Keeper Fest 25 Jan – 1 Feb. Steam lists no Autumn Sale in 2026.
February 2027 Next Fest (22 Feb – 1 Mar) deadlines: **register by 10 Jan**, submit the demo and store page by 18 Jan for Press Preview, everything by 1 Feb. This does not affect us; our plan targets June or October 2027.

## Implications for Outpost Company
1. **Melvor 2 now has the RuneScape name and launches Early Access in 3 weeks.** That raises the trademark risk of any store copy like "for fans of RuneScape". Lean on the run-and-draft hook and get a lawyer to check any comparison line before the Steam page.
2. **Melvor 2 pays the same offline as online. We pay active players 1.5–2× more.** Players coming from Melvor may read that as being punished for idling. Our copy should present mini-games as a bonus on top, never as a loss for idle players. Pillar 1 must hold in practice, not just on paper.
3. **Watch the Melvor 2 Early Access reviews in the week of 17 Nov.** What players praise and complain about (price, UI clicks, "clone" fatigue) is free research for our Early Access pitch.
