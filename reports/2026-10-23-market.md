# Market watch — 23 October 2026

First weekly watch. Baseline is `docs/research/market_research.md` (22 Oct). Only news from the past week is below.

## Biggest news: Melvor Idle 2 has a public demo
- The developers' own post (19 Oct) says the Melvor Idle 2 demo is live on Steam for Next Fest and for a few weeks after it. It has 21–22 of the alpha's 25 skills, capped at level 60, plus Act 1 of the story as quests. Demo saves carry over to Early Access. The team says it is "still on track" for Early Access in 2026. No price or date yet.
- It was the **#3 most-played demo of the fest**, about 6,700 concurrent players on 22 Oct, peak about 7,100 (OutputLag hourly chart).
- The post mentions no roguelite, run or draft features. On the Steam community hub, players worry about delays, about "melvor clones" crowding the genre, and about how many clicks the UI takes.

## Next Fest (19–26 Oct): idle demos did well, and the field is flooded
- Idle and incremental demos took 3 of the top 4 spots: Find The Needle (#2), Melvor Idle 2 (#3) and Character House, a desktop companion (#4). Many more appear in the top 140, including Idlescape (#100), a skilling MMO idler.
- About 7,200–8,000 demos took part, nearly twice June's count. GamingOnLinux counted 1,054+ with an AI disclosure. Press coverage centres on "AI slop" and AI clones of original demos.
- An incremental demo roundup lists several **run-based** incrementals launching in November: Kick the Pinata (12 Nov, rebirth upgrades), Pidgeon Pocket (9 Nov, the park changes each run) and Mr. Mageboy (18 Nov, gathering trips through a portal). **LumberBoy** (January 2027) is closest to us: chop trees, process logs, and get back to the truck before the run ends or lose the wood.
- New idlers this week include Smokestack Valley: Idle Tycoon. Its factories start in an 1890s warehouse, but it is a tycoon prestige game and not close to our hook (IdleDB). No reviews yet.

## Competitors

## Implications for Outpost Company
1. **Melvor Idle 2 will set the bar and pull the audience in 2026–27.** Our Early Access window will probably land near its Early Access. Do not compete on skill count. Lead with what it lacks: **every skill is a run with drafted, rule-changing perks.** That should be the first thing a demo or clip shows.
2. **"Run-based incremental" is no longer rare, and logging is taken as a theme** (LumberBoy, Log Drop). Our difference is per-skill prestige runs inside a skilling game, plus the 1890 expedition setting. Recommend the design reviewer checks that the Logging run reads differently from a "return to the truck" timer run.
3. **Discovery is getting harder and AI suspicion is rising.** The October fest doubled in size, and AI-disclosed games draw backlash. This supports human-made capsule and key art, and building wishlists before entering Next Fest (June or October 2027, as already planned).
