# Market watch — 23 October 2026

First weekly watch. The baseline is `docs/research/market_research.md` (22 Oct); this report covers only what is new.

## Melvor Idle 2 has a public demo
- The demo went live on Steam for Next Fest (19 Oct). It has about 21 skills capped at level 60 and Act 1 of the story, and demo saves carry over. The developers say Early Access is still planned for 2026. No price or date yet. No run or draft features are mentioned.
- It was the #3 most-played demo of the fest, with about 6,700 concurrent players (peak about 7,100).
- On the game's Steam forum, players worry about delays, about too many "melvor clones", and about how many clicks the UI needs.

## Next Fest: idle demos did well in a crowded field
- Idle demos took 3 of the top 4 spots: Find The Needle, Melvor Idle 2 and Character House. Idlescape, a skilling MMO idler, ranked about #100.
- About 7,200–8,000 demos took part, nearly twice June's count, and 1,000+ carried an AI disclosure. Press coverage is dominated by complaints about "AI slop" and AI clones.
- Run-based incrementals launching in November include Kick the Pinata (12 Nov), Pidgeon Pocket (9 Nov) and Mr. Mageboy (18 Nov). **LumberBoy** (January 2027) is closest to us: you chop and process logs, and lose any wood you have not carried back to the truck when the run ends.
- Smokestack Valley: Idle Tycoon launched this week. It opens in an 1890s warehouse, but it is a tycoon game, not a skilling game.
- Nothing new from Idle Clans and nothing notable on mobile that I could check (the mobile roundup page did not load).

## Implications for Outpost Company
1. **Melvor Idle 2 will set the bar for the genre in 2026–27, and our Early Access may launch close to its own.** We should not compete on skill count. We should lead with what it lacks: every skill is a run with drafted perks that change the rules.
2. **Run-based incrementals are no longer rare, and logging is already a theme** (LumberBoy, Log Drop). The design reviewer should check that our Logging run reads clearly differently from a "get back to the truck before time runs out" run.
3. **Getting noticed is harder, and players are suspicious of AI.** This supports human-made capsule art and building wishlists before we enter Next Fest (June or October 2027, as planned).
