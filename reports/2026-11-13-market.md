# Market watch — 13 November 2026

New since 6 Nov only. Melvor 2's Steam page, the Rogueliker list and one mobile roundup were blocked, so coverage there is thin.

## Melvor Idle 2: four days out, price still unconfirmed
- Early Access is still set for **17 November**. No official price found. The $14.99 figure traces back to fan and third-party guesses, not to Games by Malcs.
- A secondhand summary of the official 29 Oct post lists the launch content: the demo plus Astrology, Herblore and Agility, a level cap of 99 (120 at 1.0), and demo saves that carry over. Mobile comes later. *Not checked against the official post.*
- Idle Clans: nothing new found this week.

## 9 Kings reached 1.0 (12 Nov)
Hooded Horse's roguelite kingdom builder, **$19.99**, unchanged at 1.0. Steam shows 92% positive from about 10.3K English reviews, and 86% of recent reviews are positive. 1.0 adds a "Joker" mode with custom decks and rules. A Japanese review says it explains lost runs poorly.

## New on Steam this week (9–15 Nov)
About 30 idle, incremental and roguelite launches; no prices or scores shown.
- **Vena: Hex Automation Roguelike** (9 Nov) launched as planned.
- **Incremental launches:** What the Buck?!, Rejected Draft, Booster Pack Heroes, Swarmbreaker, 500 Finger Fillet and We March as One, plus cozy idlers.
- **Demo to watch:** *Game About Botting in an MMORPG* (YaxWorks), an idle strategy game about running bots in a fake MMO. It aims at the same "MMO skilling nostalgia" audience as we do.

## Implications for Outpost Company
1. **Melvor 2 launches with a demo's worth of skills plus three more and a level cap of 99, with 29 skills planned. We will launch with 3.** Players will compare skill counts, so our Early Access pitch should sell *depth per skill*: every run differs, with drafted perks. It should not sell breadth.
2. **9 Kings shows that players pay $19.99 for a run-based builder with persistent growth when the builds are strong.** Our $7.99 price is safe but may be modest. Its review complaints (players can't tell why a run was lost) are a cheap lesson: our end-of-run screen should show clearly why camp closed early. *(Price stays the owner's call; no change proposed now.)*
3. **"Game About Botting in an MMORPG" targets the same nostalgia we do.** Check its store page, tags and demo reception in the next watch, alongside Melvor 2's real price and first reviews (20 Nov).
