# Market watch — 13 November 2026

Covers only what is new since the 6 November watch. Melvor Idle 2's Steam page and the Rogueliker November list were blocked again this run. The mobile idle roundup also failed, so mobile coverage is thin.

## Melvor Idle 2: four days out, price still unconfirmed
- Early Access is still set for **17 November**. No official price found. The $14.99 figure traces back to fan and third-party guesses, not to Games by Malcs.
- A secondhand summary of the official 29 Oct post lists the launch content: everything in the demo plus Astrology, Herblore and Agility, a level cap of 99 (120 at 1.0), Act 2 of the story, and demo saves that carry over. Mobile comes after Steam Early Access, with no date. *Not checked against the official post itself.*
- Idle Clans: nothing new found this week.

## 9 Kings reached 1.0 (12 Nov)
A roguelite kingdom builder from Hooded Horse. It costs **$19.99** and the price did not rise at 1.0. Steam shows 92% positive from about 10.3K English reviews, and 86% of recent reviews are positive. 1.0 adds a "Joker" mode with custom decks and rules. A Japanese review praises the build synergies but says the game explains lost runs poorly.

## Implications for Outpost Company
1. **Melvor 2 launches with a demo's worth of skills plus three more and a level cap of 99, with 29 skills planned. We will launch with 3.** Players will compare skill counts, so our Early Access pitch should sell *depth per skill*: every run differs, with drafted perks. It should not sell breadth. Plan the store copy around "replay a skill", not "unlock many skills".
2. **9 Kings shows that players pay $19.99 for a run-based builder with persistent growth when the builds are strong.** Our $7.99 price is safe but may be modest. Its review complaints (players can't tell why a run was lost) are a cheap lesson: our end-of-run screen should show clearly why camp closed early. *(Price stays the owner's call; no change proposed now.)*
3. **"Game About Botting in an MMORPG" targets the same nostalgia we do.** Check its store page, tags and demo reception in the next watch, alongside Melvor 2's real price and first reviews (20 Nov).
