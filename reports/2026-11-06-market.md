# Market watch — 6 November 2026

Covers only what is new since the 30 October watch. This run could not open Steam store pages, Steamworks or IdleDB (blocked), so prices and review scores are missing and mobile coverage is thin.

## Melvor Idle 2: no new facts
Still Early Access on **17 November**. No official price found this week. A fan wiki lists $14.99, but it was last checked on 20 Oct and is unofficial, so treat it as unconfirmed. Nothing new from Idle Clans: the newest update article found dates from 2024.

## New this week on Steam (2–6 Nov)
Idle and incremental launches were mostly small. None had review scores on the pages opened.
- **Wielders of the Essence** (Lavapotion, 5 Nov): horde survival with incremental base building. The clearest "run plus persistent base" mix this week.
- **How Empires Work** (Crow Cottage, 3 Nov): tagged Strategy, Incremental, Historical. A history-themed incremental, so it is a neighbour to our 1890 setting.
- **Dirt Clicker** (idler/automation), **Mycelarium** (incremental exploration), **Tenebrarium** (idle colony sim), **Heaven Miner**, **PlinkIdle** and **Potato Please**: the usual mix of clickers, miners and cozy idlers.
- **The Miner Was Replaced** (2 Nov): a programming and automation game, a follow-up in spirit to The Farmer Was Replaced.
- Roguelite deckbuilders keep flooding in: about 8 launched this week (Lucky Punk, Diecast, Slumber Realm, Bibidi Bibidi!, Grimoire of Blanks, Fathom Reach EA, Sushiamo, Corpus et Spiritus).

## Coming up (rest of November)
- **Vena** (9 Nov): an automation game with roguelike runs.

## Implications for Outpost Company
1. **"Roguelite plus incremental" is no longer a rare pitch.** This month alone, Wielders, Vena and the Ball X Pit expansion all pair short runs with a base that keeps growing. Our hook should lead with what they lack: *skilling*, and *prestige for each skill separately* so a reset never stops everything. "Idle roguelite" alone will not stand out.
2. **A historical incremental now exists (How Empires Work).** Before we write Steam copy, compare its store page and tags with ours so the 1890 chartered-company setting reads as clearly different. A quick task for the next run, once Steam pages load.
3. **Melvor 2's Early Access price is still unconfirmed.** If it lands near $15, our planned $7.99 Early Access price is about half and makes a good impulse buy. Check the real price and the first reviews in the 20 Nov watch before pricing work goes further.
