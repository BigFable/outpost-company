# Market watch: 20 November 2026

This report covers only what is new since 13 Nov. I didn't check mobile this week, and the full Steam release calendars for 16–29 Nov could only be partly read.

## Melvor Idle 2 is out: $14.99 in Early Access
- It launched on **17 Nov** (developer Games by Malcs, publisher Jagex). The price is **$14.99 / £12.99 / €14.49**, with 10% off until 24 Nov. Steam says the price won't change at 1.0, and that Early Access should take 12–18 months.
- Content: 29 skills planned (five of them new), a level cap of 120, a new Quest system, reworked Mastery, and game modes with selectable challenge levels. It runs on Windows, Mac and Linux, and mobile comes later.
- Reception: **Very Positive**. The store showed 91% of 564 reviews positive; Nerdly reported 92% of 613 on 20 Nov. Nerdly saw about 20,000 players in-game three days in, but the game was not in Steam's weekly top 100.
- This settles last week's open question: the $14.99 guess was right.

## New and upcoming idle and roguelite games
- **Click to Medieval Roguelite** (Day 1 to 100), out 26 Nov. You click to add workers, then hire auto-workers, then sell the settlement and restart with upgrades. That "sell the outpost and restart" loop is close to our closing camp, but it has no skills and no perk draft. Price not shown.
- **Dawn of Defense** (17 Nov) mixes RTS, tower defense and roguelite progression. **Burning Sword: Death Sun** (17 Nov, Early Access) is a wuxia action game with roguelite progression. Neither is a skilling game.
- **Game About Botting in an MMORPG** (YaxWorks / 2 Left Thumbs): the free demo has been out since 9 Nov. It is rated Positive, 87% of 16 reviews. In the demo you automate an MMO character with "bot modules" (3 classes, about an hour each). It is tagged Idler, Incremental and Automation. The full game's price and date are not on the demo page.
- Idle Clans: nothing new found.

## Implications for Outpost Company
1. **The genre leader now costs $14.99 in Early Access, with 29 skills planned and a 12–18 month road to 1.0.** At $7.99 with 3 skills, we are clearly the cheaper game with less in it. That works only if the pitch is "runs, not more skills": drafted perks and a different outpost every run. *(The price stays the owner's call. No change is proposed.)*
2. **Melvor 2 was reviewed well but did not make Steam's weekly top 100.** Skilling idlers have a loyal but modest audience. Our wishlist plan should also reach roguelite players (fans of Balatro and 9 Kings), not only Melvor fans.
3. **Click to Medieval Roguelite, out 26 Nov, uses a "sell the settlement and restart" loop.** Next week, check its price and first reviews to see how players react to it.
