## Bugs

### QA-01 (major): Space closes Saw for grade instead of sawing
- **Repro:** have a Sawmill, click "Saw for grade", press `1` to pick a log, press `Space` (the button is labelled "Saw a board (Space)"). Real Chromium: the game shuts and the log is gone (see QA-02).
- **Expected:** Space saws a board. **Actual:** the focused X (`#mgClose`) is pressed.
- **Cause:** `openMG` focuses `#mgClose` (line 5844). The keydown handler passes `onButton = true` because the focused element is a button (line 5874), and `sawKey` only treats Space as "saw" when `!onButton` (line 7245), so nothing calls `preventDefault` and the browser activates the focused button. `sawPick` rebuilds the buttons but never moves focus. Also reachable with the mouse: pick a log by clicking it (pointerdown calls `preventDefault`, so focus stays on the X), then press Space.
- **Fix idea:** move focus to `#sSaw` in `sawPick`/`sawButtons`, or treat Space as saw when the target is `#mgClose` and call `preventDefault`.
- **Repro test:** `tests/repro/repro_01_saw_space_closes_game.js`. Fell, skid and haul are not affected (they call `preventDefault` on Space).

### QA-02 (major): picking a log spends the Prime spar and pond logs before any board is sawn
- **Repro:** bank 3 Prime spars, open Saw for grade, pick a Prime log, press Done / Escape / X (or reload). Spars go 3 to 2, no lumber, no refund. Same for the 240 bd ft of pond logs on an ordinary log.
- **Cause:** `sawPick` (line 7187) deducts `S.bank[SPAR]` / `S.bulk.logs` / cove cash immediately and sets `m.bigStick`; `closeMG` does not refund. Combined with QA-01 this is easy to hit by accident; Prime spars gate the Admiralty order and the mill structures.
- **Fix idea:** deduct in `sawFinish`, or refund in `closeMG` when `W.phase === 'saw'` and no board has been sawn.
- **Repro test:** `repro_02_saw_pick_spends_before_sawing.js`.

### QA-03 (major, needs a human check in a hidden tab): idle progress is lost when timer callbacks are far apart
- **Repro (deterministic):** let 10 minutes pass between two timer callbacks (laptop sleep, frozen tab, Chrome's intensive throttling of a tab hidden for more than 5 minutes, one callback a minute). The game advances 5 s. With Playwright's `clock.fastForward(30 min)`: 6 game seconds.
- **Cause:** line 7598: `dt = Math.min(5, ...)` and `offline()` runs only at page load (line 7551). `visibilitychange` only saves. `docs/research/tech_plan.md` line 75 says the sim "runs on wall-clock deltas" and line 131 describes catch-up by elapsed time. The prototype does not do either while the page stays loaded.
- **Impact:** pillar 1 ("idle is always enough") in a browser tab: a player who leaves the tab in the background gets a fraction of the progress a reload would give. Electron may behave differently if `backgroundThrottling` is off; not verified.
- **Fix idea:** when `now - prev > 5`, call the offline path with the gap (it is already tested against online play), or sub-step the loop.
- **Repro test:** `repro_03_loop_caps_dt_so_sleep_loses_time.js` (fakes `performance.now`).

### QA-09 (major, balance and design, owner call): the Storm season curse is nearly free, and Ill wind makes it a net bonus
- **Numbers** (mean stage factor over a full year of the 16-event tables, versus a quiet year; the no-two-bad-in-a-row rule only lowers the cost further):

  | Setup | Speed vs. plain table |
  | --- | --- |
  | Storm season | 0.992 (about -0.9%) |
  | Ill wind alone | 1.025 |
  | Storm season + Ill wind | 1.034 |
  | Storm signals | about 1.0 |

  Storm season adds +2% Charter Shares per weathered bad event up to +30% (`stormMult`, line 6663; counted in `weatherTurn`, line 5324), outside the order cap. Bad events are small, so the curse's cost is under 1%, against the design's own allowance of up to 8% for a Storm season outpost (seasonal-events.md section 7). With Ill wind the bad events speed crews up (Blizzard first half: 1.6 on skid and haul), so the "curse" pays shares and speed.
- **Also:** `stormN` counts a bad event even when the payroll guard paused it or Ill wind inverted it.
- **Recommendation:** raise the curse's bite (for example bad-event penalties 1.5x, or an upkeep cost) or lower the share step; and have Ill wind not count toward `stormN`. Balance agent to rerun after any change.
- **Repro test:** `repro_09_storm_season_curse_is_free.js`.

### Minors

- **QA-04: close camp resets the calendar year to 1890.** `closeCamp`'s `keep` list (line 6734) has `season` and `st` but not `year`. Design says the calendar survives close camp. Effects: almanac "first seen" labels repeat, log stamps go backwards. Test: `repro_04_close_camp_resets_year.js`.
- **QA-05: `fmt` rounds after picking the suffix** (line 5074). `fmt(999.6)` prints "1000", `fmt(999999)` "1000K", `fmt(999999999)` "1000M". Past 1e18 it prints "1000Qa", and `fmt(1e21)` prints "1000000Qa" (no suffix beyond Qa). Test: `repro_05_fmt_rollover.js`.
- **QA-06: `buildRoad()` on a tract without `roadCost` sets cash to NaN** (line 5157-5159: `S.cash < undefined` is false). The Cove woodlot and tracts from old saves have no `roadCost`. The button is hidden for them (line 5633), so not reachable today; one NaN and every cash comparison in `tick`, payroll and buying goes wrong (JSON then saves it as null). `normalize` does not sanitise numbers either: a save with `"cash":null` keeps `S.cash = null`, `"cash":"abc"` keeps a string. Test: `repro_06_build_road_without_cost_makes_cash_nan.js`.
- **QA-07: warehouse Lock does not stop "Sell 1" / "Sell all"** (lines 7416, 7455). Only Sell surplus honours it. Test: `repro_07_sell_all_ignores_lock.js`.
- **QA-08: a corrupt or non-v2 save is silently replaced and then overwritten.** `load()` (line 7548) falls back to a fresh company, and the 5-second autosave writes over the old text; no backup key. Test: `repro_08_corrupt_save_is_overwritten.js`.
- **QA-10: offline catch-up is clamped to 4 hours** (line 7551) and the "While you were away" note reports the clamped time ("4 h") without saying so. A player away 8 h is told 4 h. Design mentions no cap for Logging. Either raise it, or say "capped at 4 h".
- **QA-11: first weather card contradicts the design.** seasonal-events.md says the very first card is always Supply ship in or Long days; the build deals the next season's friendly card (a new company starts in Fall, so Ice bridge; `FIRST_CARD` in weather.ts, and `smoke_seasons.js` asserts it). Decide which is right and fix the text or the code.
- **QA-12: hand-edited or damaged `runs` entries crash the whole UI.** `renderRun` reads `x.mods.length` and `ERAS[x.era].name` (line 6900) with no validation in `normalize`. A save with `runs:[{n:1}]` throws in `boot()` and the page stays blank. Not reachable by normal play (every closed camp since the first commit writes both fields).
- **QA-13: an unclicked first-sighting weather card blocks every later card.** `pumpDispatch` only advances when `dCur` is null and sticky cards never time out (line 5706), so `dispQ` grows by one per season until the player clicks. The season sub-line still shows the weather, so this is cosmetic.
