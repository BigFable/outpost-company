      How to cut logs
      1. Pick a spot. Orange marks show where a log can end: 8, 10, 12, 14 or 16 ft from your last cut.
      2. Check the color. Blue is a Fine log (2 diamonds), green is Good (knots, 1 diamond), grey is Common (bend), brown dashes are firewood. Tap or click to cut.
      3. Send it. Cut off the rot and keep knots and bends out of your best logs. A top under 8 in is firewood. Stuck? Hint shows the next best cut, for a few points.
