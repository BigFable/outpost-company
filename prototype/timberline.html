    How to buck a stem
    1. Point along the stem. The orange marks show where a sawlog can end: 8, 10, 12, 14 or 16 ft from your last cut.
    2. Read the piece before you cut. It turns green for a grade 1 sawlog, dark for grade 2 (knots), amber for grade 3 (sweep) and grey for pulp, with the reason and what it is worth. Tap or click to cut; arrow keys and Enter work too.
    3. Send it to the landing. Cut the rot off the butt, keep knots and sweep out of your best logs, and leave the top under 8 in as pulp. Stuck? Suggest a cut shows the next cut of the best plan, for a few points off the score.
