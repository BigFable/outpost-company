    Special orders

    Filled from the company warehouse. Each one adds Charter Shares when you close camp (at most +25% an outpost) and unlocks a perk for your drafts. Deadlines count outposts, not minutes; a missed order costs nothing and comes back two outposts later.

    Cut for hire

    The buyer supplies the timber, so there's nothing to buy. You're paid a contract rate of 45% of market value for what you deliver, plus an advance when you sign. One job at a time.

    Charter orders

    A premium on everything you haul until the order is filled. Miss the sailing date and you pay a penalty for the share you didn't deliver.

    How to buck a stem
    1. Point along the stem. The orange marks show where a sawlog can end: 8, 10, 12, 14 or 16 ft from your last cut.
    2. Read the piece before you cut. It turns green for a grade 1 sawlog, dark for grade 2 (knots), amber for grade 3 (sweep) and grey for pulp, with the reason and what it is worth. Tap or click to cut; arrow keys and Enter work too.
    3. Send it to the landing. Cut the rot off the butt, keep knots and sweep out of your best logs, and leave the top under 8 in as pulp. Stuck? Suggest a cut shows the next cut of the best plan, for a few points off the score.
